the algorithm achieves positive expectancy (Sharpe > 0.5).

📞 COMMON QUESTIONS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How do I log a new backtest?
A: Add a row to backtest_log.csv with the metrics. See README.md.

Q: What's the difference between Gross and Net Sharpe?
A: Gross = before spread costs | Net = after spread costs (use Net!)

Q: How do I know if my change improved things?
A: Compare your new row to the previous row in backtest_log.csv.
   If Net Sharpe increased, your change helped!

Q: What's the Python API for?
A: Automate logging if you're running many backtests programmatically.
   Manual CSV editing works fine for < 10 runs/day.

Q: Why did the initial backtest fail so badly?
A: See VALIDATION_REPORT_2026-03-22.md, Section IV: Diagnosis.
   TL;DR: Bad signals, overtrading, short training window.

Q: Is this ready for live trading?
A: No! This is for backtesting only. Needs Phase 4 (risk management)
   and Phase 5 (shadow trading) before live deployment.

Q: How many iterations until the strategy works?
A: Estimated 5-10 iterations based on recommended changes:
   1. Thresholds
   2. Training window
   3. Labels
   4. Features
   5. Regime gating
